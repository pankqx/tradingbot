# Product spec (summary)

Goal: help a user discover relevant stocks, understand why they qualify, inspect signals with their evidence, learn, and practise without risking money. Not a broker; no order execution.

Principles: personalisation over generic lists; evidence over prediction; plain language; risk visibility; measured performance over claimed accuracy.

Navigation: Overview, Discover stocks, Signals, Charts, Watchlists, Paper trading, Backtesting, Learn, Journal, Settings. Unbuilt areas link to an honest roadmap page rather than a mock screen.

Status by capability is tracked in `TASKS.md`. The full original brief covers onboarding, backtesting, paper trading, the academy, security and compliance; those are scheduled in Milestones 2 to 6.
